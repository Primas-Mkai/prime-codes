# Menos Academy

Parent Handbook 2027

Private primary school, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania

Demo document: Menos Academy is a fictional school created for portfolio purposes. All names, fees, dates and contact details are invented.

## 1. About the school

Menos Academy is a private English-medium primary school in Dar es Salaam. We teach children from Nursery (age 4) to Standard 7. Each class has a maximum of 28 pupils and a class teacher, supported by a teaching assistant in Nursery to Standard 2. Swahili is taught as a subject every day, and English is the language of instruction for all other subjects.

The Head Teacher is Mrs. Neema Kileo. The school is led by a small management team made up of the Head Teacher, the Academic Coordinator and the Accounts Officer. This handbook explains how the school works day to day. If something changes during the year, parents will be told in writing through the class group on WhatsApp and by email.

## 3. Fees and payment

### 3.1 Tuition fees for 2027

Fees are charged per term in Tanzanian shillings (TZS). The yearly total is the sum of three terms.

| **Class**       | **Per term (TZS)** | **Yearly (TZS)** | **Notes**          |
|-----------------|--------------------|------------------|--------------------|
| Nursery         | 1,200,000          | 3,600,000        | Half-day programme |
| Standard 1 to 4 | 1,500,000          | 4,500,000        | Full day           |
| Standard 5 to 7 | 1,700,000          | 5,100,000        | Full day           |

### 3.2 Other charges

- Registration fee: TZS 100,000, paid once when a child joins. It is not refundable.

- Lunch (optional): TZS 250,000 per term. Lunch is cooked at the school and the menu changes every week.

- Aftercare (optional drop-in): TZS 5,000 per day. See section 5.

- Textbooks and exercise books are included in tuition. Pupils bring their own stationery.

### 3.3 When and how to pay

Term fees are due by the end of the first week of each term (the Friday of week 1). Payment is made by bank transfer or at the school bank branch using the invoice sent by the Accounts Officer. The invoice shows the account details and a reference number which must be used for every payment. The school office does not accept cash.

Parents may pay in two instalments if they ask the Accounts Officer in writing before the due date. The first instalment is 60% of the term fee, due in week 1, and the second is 40%, due by the end of week 6. Instalments are not available for the registration fee.

### 3.4 Late payment

If a term fee is still unpaid 14 days after the due date, a late fee of TZS 50,000 is added to the invoice. The late fee is charged once per term. A child may be asked to stay at home from the start of week 5 if the fees are unpaid and no payment plan has been agreed with the Accounts Officer.

### 3.5 Sibling discount

Families with more than one child at the school receive a discount on tuition fees: 10% off the second child and 15% off the third and any further child. The discount applies to tuition only, not to the registration fee, lunch or aftercare. The eldest child pays the full fee.

### 3.6 Leaving the school and refunds

Parents who plan to take a child out of the school must give 30 days written notice to the Head Teacher. If the notice is given after a term has started, the fees for that term remain payable. Fees paid for a term that has started are not refunded.

## 5. School day, arrival and collection

### 5.1 School hours

|               | **Nursery** | **Standard 1 to 7** |
|---------------|-------------|---------------------|
| Gates open    | 7:30        | 6:45                |
| Lessons start | 8:00        | 7:30                |

Pupils who arrive after lessons have started must report to the front office to be signed in. A child who arrives late more than three times in one term will lead to a call from the class teacher to the parents.

### 5.2 Collection and late pickup

Children are only handed to adults named on the collection form. Everyone collecting a child must show an identity card to the gate officer the first time and when the gate officer does not know them. To add or change a person on the list, parents must give the front office a written note or send an email from the address held on file; a phone call alone is not enough.

After dismissal, children who have not been collected wait in the supervised waiting area until 15:00 at no charge. From 15:00 to 17:00 the child joins aftercare, which costs TZS 5,000 per day. Aftercare closes at 17:00. After 17:00 a late collection charge of TZS 10,000 is added for every 15 minutes or part of 15 minutes. If a child has not been collected by 18:00 and the school cannot reach the parents or any other person on the collection form, the Head Teacher will decide what to do to make sure the child is safe.

## 6. Attendance, absence and sickness

- If your child will be absent, call or message the school office before 8:00 on the first day of absence. State the child's name, class and the reason.

- For an absence of three or more school days because of illness, the school asks for a note from a doctor or clinic when the child returns.

- For planned absences such as family travel, send a written request to the Head Teacher at least five school days in advance. Approval is not guaranteed, and the school does not provide work in advance for holidays taken during term.

- Children with a fever, vomiting or diarrhoea must stay at home until they have been free of symptoms for 24 hours.

- For contagious illnesses such as chickenpox or measles, please tell the office on the first day. The school will advise on when the child may return.

The school nurse is on duty from 7:30 to 15:00 on school days. If a child becomes unwell at school, the nurse or class teacher calls the first contact on the form, who must collect the child as soon as possible. The nurse gives medicine only when the parent has completed the medication form and handed in the medicine in its original packaging with the child's name on it.

## 7. Behaviour and discipline

We expect pupils to be respectful, honest and kind. Each class agrees its rules at the start of the year, and good behaviour is recognised with weekly certificates and a termly award. The school does not use corporal punishment under any circumstances.

When a pupil breaks the rules, the school follows these steps:

- Step 1: a verbal warning from the teacher.

- Step 2: a written note in the pupil's school diary, which parents must sign.

- Step 3: loss of break time privileges, supervised by a teacher, and a phone call to the parents.

- Step 4: a meeting with the parents and the Academic Coordinator, and a written behaviour plan.

- Step 5: suspension of up to three school days for serious or repeated problems.

Bullying, fighting, theft, bringing dangerous items to school and leaving the school grounds without permission are treated as serious cases and may go straight to Step 4 or Step 5. Pupils are not allowed to bring mobile phones to school. A phone that is found will be taken by the teacher and returned only to a parent in person.

## 8. School transport

Menos Academy runs a school bus service on three routes in Dar es Salaam. Places on the buses are limited and are given in the order that applications are received. Parents who want a place must apply to the transport office before the start of the term. The transport office confirms the route, the pick-up stops and the price for each term in a letter. Buses are accompanied by a school attendant, and every child must wear a seat belt.

Parents who bring and collect their children by car must use the drop-off lane at the main gate and must not park on the road in front of the school.

## 9. Lunch and snacks

Pupils who are signed up for lunch eat in the dining hall at 12:30 (Nursery) or 12:45 (Standard 1 to 7). A weekly menu is sent to parents on Fridays. Pupils who do not take the school lunch must bring a packed lunch. The school has a no-nuts rule: please do not send food containing peanuts or other nuts. A short morning snack break takes place at 10:00; pupils bring their own snack and a water bottle. Sweets and fizzy drinks are not allowed.

If your child has a food allergy, tell the class teacher and the school nurse in writing and complete the medical form.

## 10. Communication and contacts

Each class has a WhatsApp group for parents, used by the class teacher for notices, homework reminders and events. The groups are not for private messages. Please contact the teacher by email or through the office for anything personal about your child. Teachers reply within two school days. Homework is given from Standard 1 and is recorded in the pupil's school diary.
