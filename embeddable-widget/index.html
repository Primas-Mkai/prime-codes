June to October is the dry season and the easiest time to see wildlife. January and February are calving season in the southern Serengeti. April and May bring the long rains, fewer visitors and lower lodge rates.

Park fees, a private vehicle with a licensed driver-guide, accommodation, meals on safari, drinking water and airport transfers. International flights, visas, tips and travel insurance are not included.
