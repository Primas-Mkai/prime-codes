How it works

From sign-up to live in three steps

  1. 1

    Create an account and get verified

    Create an account on the website, enter your business details and upload verification documents. This check is called KYC ("know your customer"): the company confirms who the business is before letting it handle money. Verification usually takes 2 to 3 working days.

  2. 2

    Try everything in test mode

    Every plan includes a free test mode (sandbox), a practice area with fake money. Developers can start in test mode straight away, before verification.

  3. 3

    Go live

    After verification, your business can go live and start accepting payments and sending payouts.

Fintech words

Plain-language glossary

Payments come with a lot of jargon. Here are the common words, explained simply.

Wallet

An account kept on a phone or app that holds money.

Mobile money

Moving and storing money using a phone number instead of a bank account.

KYC

Identity checks a company must do before handling someone's money. It stands for "know your customer".

API

A way for two programs to talk to each other, so a website can ask Menos Pay to collect a payment without a person clicking around.

Sandbox / test mode

A practice area with fake money.

Settlement

The moment the money Menos Pay collected is sent to the business's own account.

Float

Money held temporarily while it moves between parties.

Chargeback

When a card customer disputes a payment with their bank and the money is pulled back.

Webhook

An automatic message Menos Pay sends to a business's system when something happens, such as "payment received".

Reconciliation

Matching your records against the payment records to check that they agree.

Payout

Money sent out from the business to someone else.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Menos Pay a real company?

No. Menos Pay is a fictional demo company made for a portfolio project. No real payments are processed.

Are failed payments charged?

No. Failed payments cost nothing.

When do I get my money?

Settlement is when Menos Pay sends the money it collected to your business account. On the Growth plan this happens the next working day (T+1). On the Starter plan it happens within 2 working days.

How long does verification take?

Verification usually takes 2 to 3 working days. Developers can start in test mode straight away, before verification.

What are the limits?

The minimum single payment is TZS 1,000 and the maximum is TZS 5,000,000. You can send up to 500 payouts in one batch.

Is there a test mode?

Yes. A free test mode (sandbox) is included on every plan, so you can try everything with fake money.

Do I get the fee back if I refund a payment?

No. The original Checkout fee is not returned on a refund.

How can my customers pay?

With Menos Checkout, customers can pay by mobile money or by card, through a payment link or through the API.
